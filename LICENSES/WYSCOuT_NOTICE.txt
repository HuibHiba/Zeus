This game uses derived numerical shot-model coefficients and aggregate targets
for contact fouls and cards. The game developers fitted models, selected the
ordinary-contact-foul category, and mapped aggregate targets to provisional
simulation settings. The original event and match files are not included.
These modifications and the simulation's behaviour are not endorsed by the
dataset authors or Wyscout. No claim of realistic prediction is made.
