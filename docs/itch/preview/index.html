About the game

Your notebook is the game. You are the gaffer in the dugout. Draw how your team builds up, presses and defends, then watch a complete 90-minute match play out in a top-down 2D view and step in as coach when the game stops.

On first start a short guided tour shows you around the main menu, and the game has music and sound effects that were all made for it (no external samples).

Every match is a full simulation of 22 players with limited vision and their own level of skill. The same seed and tactics reproduce the same match, so you can test an idea, change one thing and see what happens.

Early work-in-progress demo. Things are unfinished and will change, and old progress may stop working after an update. All clubs, players and seasons are fictional.

Controls

1 4 8Match speed
EndCalculate the rest of the match quickly
EscSettings: match length, music and effects volume, language, window, help, feedback
F1 / ?Explain the current screen

Keyboard and mouse. No gamepad support yet. A standard match takes about 4 to 5 real minutes.

FAQ and known limitations

Is this finished?

No. It is a preliminary demo. Features, menus and balance will change.

Why do players sometimes stand still or run oddly?

The player AI is still rough. Improving it is the first item on the roadmap.

Are the results realistic?

Calibration is provisional, especially at amateur level. Treat results as the behaviour of this simulation, not as a statement about real football.

Are the clubs and players real?

No. All clubs, players and seasons are fictional.

Does it send any data?

No. The game never uses the network. The F9 feedback file is created on your PC and you decide whether to send it. Your Windows user name is removed from file paths.

Where does the sound come from?

The music and effects are generated for this game, with no external samples or recordings. They are simple and still basic. Volume sliders are in Settings, and the About screen lists all credits.

Will my progress survive updates?

Not guaranteed during the demo phase.

How can I give feedback?

Leave a comment on this itch page. Please do not post feedback zips or screenshots publicly.
