A football tactics sandbox: draw your plan in a notebook, then coach the match.

Download demo (Windows)

Your notebook is the game

You are the manager in the dugout. Set up formations, roles, pressing and team shape in a tactic editor, then watch a complete 90-minute match play out in a top-down 2D view. Step in as coach when the game stops.

Early work-in-progress demo. Things are unfinished and will change. Please read the known limitations below.

What can you do in half an hour?

  1. Play a live challenge with a concrete goal, such as scoring against a low block.
  2. Coach: press B to swap players or change tactic at the next stoppage.
  3. Draw your own tactic in the Tactic Maker, per phase of play.
  4. Compare two tactics in the Tactic Meter over many simulated matches.
  5. Play a league match in a small fictional league.

Controls

SpacePause / resume

Windows 10/11 64-bit, OpenGL 3.3, about 70 MB download. Unzip and run GaffersNotebook.exe. No account, no internet, no telemetry.

Known limitations

  • Preliminary demo: features, menus and balance will change.
  • The player AI is still rough; players can stand still or run oddly.
  • Calibration is provisional, especially at amateur level. Results say nothing about real football.
  • All clubs, players and seasons are fictional.
  • Windows may show "Unknown publisher" because the build is not code-signed.
