# Concepttekst: eerste devlog en uitnodiging proeftester (voor de maker, nog niets geplaatst)

Interne concepten, Engels waar ze voor spelers bedoeld zijn. Niets hiervan is geplaatst of verstuurd; de maker beslist wanneer en waar. Hoort bij roadmap-onderdelen A1 (speltest ronde 1) en SJ12 (Discord en devlogs), zie [roadmap-naar-1.0.md](roadmap-naar-1.0.md). Alleen opnemen wat in de gedeelde demo (0.1.0-beta.1) zit of echt in 0.2 komt; de lijst "komt in 0.2" is een voorstel en moet bij de release worden gecontroleerd.

## 1. Uitnodiging voor de eerste proeftester (Discord-DM of bericht)

> Hi! I'm making a football tactics game called Gaffer's Notebook and the first demo is out. I'd like you to be my first tester. It takes about 30 minutes: install, play one match, and tell me what confused you and what was fun. Download: https://trainertoto.itch.io/gaffersnotebook (Windows, free, about 100 MB; Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the game is not signed yet).
> If something goes wrong, press F9 in the game: it saves a small zip with a screenshot and the game log on your desktop. Nothing is sent automatically, you can send it to me if you want.
> Three questions I'd love an answer to: (1) What did you think you had to do in the first 5 minutes? (2) Did a match feel too long, too short, or fine? (3) Would you play another one tomorrow? Thanks!

## 2. Devlog 1 (itch en Discord #devlog), concept

**Devlog 1: what's next**

The first demo (0.1.0-beta.1) is out and free on itch. Thanks to everyone who tried it. This is what I'm working on for 0.2:

- Fixing what testers find confusing or broken (the top 10 list comes from your feedback).
- Making players move more like a real team: the block shifting sideways with the ball and fewer players standing still when they don't have the ball.
- Better level balance now that offside is handled properly.
- Smarter opponents in the league (they adapt to a style you repeat) and a first version of corner and free-kick routines in the tactic maker.

Not promised, just the direction: a bigger world with more leagues and a coach you create yourself, further out. Feedback goes to #feedback and #bug-reports on Discord, or the itch comments.
